Dane służą świadczeniu usług reklamowych, pomiarowi i ograniczaniu nadużyć. Reklamę nagradzaną uruchamia wybór gracza. Gra nie żąda uprawnień do lokalizacji.

Gameplay and saves

Saves, backups, progress and settings stay on the device. The game uses Android’s clock, elapsed boot time and boot count to calculate elapsed time. The optional activity log keeps up to 128 entries about app actions locally. The publisher does not receive these entries. Disabling the log clears the entries. Clearing the save in Android settings also clears local progress.

Ads and consent

This version uses test Google Mobile Ads units and User Messaging Platform (UMP). UMP checks consent choices and can show a form. When Google makes them available, privacy options can be reopened in game settings. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from the IP address, device or app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. It uses this information to provide and measure advertising and prevent abuse. Players choose whether to start a rewarded ad. The game does not request location permission.

Google describes its processing, retention and privacy choices in its documentation. Google Privacy Policy · Google partner services

Payments

Closed beta does not start payments or purchase sign-in. The app does not send purchase tokens to a verifier. The campaign and in-game rewards are available without a publisher account.
