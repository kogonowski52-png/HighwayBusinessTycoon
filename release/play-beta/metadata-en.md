# English Google Play listing

## Title

Highway Business Tycoon

## Short description

Run a gas station, manage your fleet and grow a roadside business empire.

## Full description

Your first customer is already waiting at the pump. Fuel the car, hire a crew and see your service station change with each upgrade. Grow at your own pace, from one small stop to a network of highway businesses.

Highway Business Tycoon brings gas station simulation and idle business management together. Watch drivers queue on the 3D map, see your crew serve customers and follow your own fleet as it heads out on a delivery.

- Unlock 12 districts and operate up to 84 businesses, including car washes, repair shops, restaurants, motels, logistics hubs and gas stations.
- Build a fleet. Buy vehicles and trailers, train drivers, maintain your trucks and choose among 60 transport contracts.
- Improve customer service. Upgrade infrastructure, assign managers and customize each site.
- Look inside selected businesses and try optional refueling, washing and cargo loading mini-games.
- Collect income from staffed businesses when you return and pursue missions, contracts and seasonal goals.

The core campaign works offline in English and Polish. Calendar rewards and missions earn in-game currency. Optional rewarded videos require an internet connection.
