## Highway Business Tycoon privacy policy

This policy was prepared on 27 November 2026 for the closed testing release of the Android app `pl.krystiandevelopment.highwaytycoon`. The publisher is Krystian Development. For privacy matters, contact [kogonowski52@gmail.com](mailto:kogonowski52@gmail.com).

### Progress, settings and local diagnostics

The app keeps campaign progress, virtual balances, settings, a recovery copy and a local installation identifier on your device. Android supplies the clock, time since boot and boot count so the app can calculate offline income and timed bonuses. The publisher does not send local saves or the activity log to its server.

The activity log is optional and off by default. When enabled, it stores up to 128 local entries about gameplay, including the action, time and the place in the app where it happened. Turning it off clears those entries. You can erase progress and settings by clearing the app’s data in Android or uninstalling it. Erasing a save also erases local progress. The publisher does not enable Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics in this release.

### Advertising, identifiers and choices

The closed release uses test Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) units and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). UMP checks your choices and can show a privacy form when required. When Google makes the form available, you can change your choices from the game settings. Core gameplay works offline. You decide whether to start a rewarded video. If the SDK can request ads, it can start preloading ads earlier.

Depending on your device, region, choices and configuration, Google SDKs may receive your IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, an advertising or other identifier, ad interactions and diagnostics, including SDK errors or crash information. This information supports ad delivery and measurement, troubleshooting and fraud prevention. The app does not request GPS access. Where applicable law requires consent for advertising data or measurement, Google reads the choice through UMP. Fraud prevention can be subject to the applicable basis described by Google. The app does not share your ordinary game progress with other players.

Google receives data when its SDKs operate. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and [How Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en) for information on Google’s retention, account controls, transfers and safeguards. Advertising identifier controls are also available where supported in Android settings.

### Purchases and accounts

This package includes Google Play Billing. In the closed configuration, real-money checkout, purchase sign-in and the purchase verifier connection are disabled. The game requires no publisher account, sends no purchase history to the verifier and receives no payment card data. Spending and earnings in the campaign are simulated. A release with active checkout requires updated information about order handling and the actual server involved.

### Contact, rights and deletion

Where applicable law gives you these rights, you may ask for access, rectification, restriction, portability or deletion, withdraw consent, object, or lodge a complaint with the appropriate supervisory authority. To make a request, contact the publisher at the address above. Include details of the request and the game version. We handle only the information needed to reply, contact you and securely confirm your entitlement when necessary. The basis is responding to your request, the applicable legitimate interest or a legal obligation. We keep support contact information for as long as needed to handle the request and meet relevant obligations. Please avoid sending Google passwords, payment card data or private keys.

To delete the local save, clear the app’s data in Android. For data processed by Google, also use Google’s privacy settings or its designated request process. The publisher cannot directly erase data from Google’s servers.
