Your first driver is waiting at the pump. Fuel the car, open another bay and turn a small gas station into a network of roadside businesses.

Highway Business Tycoon blends a gas station simulator with idle business management. In a stylized 3D world, watch drivers choose services, crews work at their stations and new buildings transform each site.

• Build and upgrade your businesses. Open car washes, repair shops, restaurants, stores, truck parking and motels. Improve your roads and customize each location.
• Expand across 12 districts and manage 84 facilities throughout the campaign. Start at Northpoint Station and grow through desert roads, coastal districts and city junctions.
• Hire and train your crew, then assign specialist managers. Collect 33 managers and see 48 car and truck models on your roads.
• Run a transport fleet. Buy vehicles and trailers, train drivers and maintain your trucks. Choose from 60 delivery contracts.
• Look inside selected businesses and try optional refueling, washing and cargo-loading mini-games. Earn rewards through missions, the calendar and seasonal events.
• Collect idle income from staffed businesses when you return. Core gameplay works offline. If you choose, watch rewarded videos for extra in-game bonuses.

Play in English or Polish. All balances and earnings are part of the simulation. The game contains ads, and advertising features require an internet connection.
