Nie zapisuje tokena tożsamości. Baza zawiera skrót tokena, stan zamówienia i powiązanie z pseudonimem konta oraz zapisem. Token potrzebny do ponowienia finalizacji jest przechowywany w postaci szyfrowanej do finalizacji lub unieważnienia. Sekretów, tokenów i treści żądań nie zapisujemy w dzienniku błędów.

Gameplay and local saves

The game stores your regions, progress, virtual currency balances, and settings on your device in a game save and its backup. To calculate BOOST time, the game reads Android’s clock, elapsed time since boot, and boot count. If you enable the activity log, it records up to 128 recent entries on the device, such as the type and time of an upgrade, an ad impression, or opening the shop. Turning the log off clears those entries. The publisher does not send them to Firebase or another analytics service. You can clear local data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Deleting your save may erase your progress and unspent virtual currency.

Advertising and privacy choices

Ad-enabled builds use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and User Messaging Platform (UMP). When the game requests ads, UMP checks your preferences and displays a form where required. The game settings let you revisit privacy choices when Google makes that form available. Depending on your region, choices, and configuration, Google may receive IP addresses, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, SDK events, and diagnostic data for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google may infer approximate location from IP addresses. The game does not ask Android for location permission. A player must choose to view a rewarded ad. The remove-interstitials purchase leaves privacy choices and optional rewarded ads available.

Purchases, sign-in, and restoration

If you choose to buy or restore items, Google Play handles payment. The publisher does not receive card details. Credential Manager sends a Google identity token with a one-time challenge to our verifier over HTTPS. The server verifies your identity and derives a pseudonymous account ID. It does not store the identity token. For purchases, the server receives the product code, purchase token, and random save identifier to fulfil orders, restore access, process revocations, and prevent token reuse. The purchase ledger retains the token hash, order state, pseudonymous account ID, and save identifier. A token needed for pending finalization is encrypted until the purchase is finalized or voided. The error log does not store secrets, tokens, or request bodies.
