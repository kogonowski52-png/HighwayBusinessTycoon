# Google Play listing · English

## Title

Highway Business Tycoon

## Short description

Turn a gas station into a highway empire in this offline idle business game.

## Full description

Your first driver is waiting at the pump. Refuel the car, collect your payment, and upgrade the station. Hire a crew as business picks up. Soon you’ll be running a whole network of roadside services.

Highway Business Tycoon blends a gas station simulator with the long-term growth of an idle business game. Follow the traffic on a 3D map as drivers pick a service, queue, and pull into open bays. Run a car wash, open a repair shop, or turn a small rest stop into a busy truck stop.

- Expand across six locations, from Northpoint Station to desert roads, a green valley, snowy mountains, the coast, and a city highway.
- Manage 41 services, including restaurants, motels, EV charging, logistics terminals, and more.
- Train employees and assign managers to serve more drivers and improve your operations.
- Complete business contracts and missions, build collections, and unlock rewards in limited-time events.
- Return to collect what staffed businesses earned while you were away.

The core campaign works offline. Optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases require a connection. You can develop every location without buying currency. Available in English and Polish.
